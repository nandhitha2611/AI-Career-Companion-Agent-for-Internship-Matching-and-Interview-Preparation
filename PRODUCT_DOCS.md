# AI Internship Agent - Product Specification & User Guide

Welcome to the AI Internship Agent! This platform streamlines the internship discovery, resume matching, ATS optimization, and application process for students and early-career developers.

---

## 1. Platform Overview & Benefits

The AI Internship Agent automates skill-matching, resume optimization, and application preparation so applicants can focus on interviewing.

* **Precision Filtering:** Automatically filters out roles requiring years of experience you do not have, surfacing only student-ready and entry-level positions.

* **Time Savings:** Saves 10+ hours weekly by automatically cross-referencing candidate skills against complex job descriptions.

* **Higher Response Rates:** Matching candidates based on high skill overlap significantly improves interview callback odds compared to blind cold applications.

* **Resume Optimization:** The ATS Resume Checker analyzes uploaded resumes and provides an ATS score and optimization report to help candidates improve their resume.

---

## 2. Navigation & Page Workflows

### Home Page

The Home Page provides an introduction to the agent's core capabilities, highlighting how skill-based matching optimizes the internship search process.

### Internships Page

The Internships Page lists all available internship opportunities on the platform.

* **Search & Filters:** Filter roles by Stipend Value, Domain, Location, Remote work status, and Part-time availability.

* **Direct Application:** Users can click on any listing to inspect details and initiate the application process directly.

### Match Internships Page

The AI-powered matching hub allows candidates to find their best-fit roles.

* **Resume Selection:** Upload a new `.pdf` or `.docx` resume, or select a previously uploaded resume.

* **Top 5 Matched Roles:** The AI matching engine analyzes skill overlap and returns the top 5 most relevant internship listings alongside an explicit Match Score percentage.

* **Application Initiation:** Users can review matched roles and apply directly from this tab.

### ATS Score Page

The ATS Score page allows users to check how well their resume is optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

* **Accessing ATS Score:** Go to the **ATS Score** tab from the main navigation.

* **Resume Selection:** Users can select a previously saved resume from the saved-resume list or upload a new `.pdf` or `.docx` resume.

* **ATS Analysis:** After selecting or uploading a resume, the user can run the ATS analysis.

* **ATS Score:** The ATS Score page displays the ATS score and the associated resume analysis/report.

* **Optimization Feedback:** The analysis provides feedback and recommendations that can help the user improve their resume for ATS compatibility.

* **File Support:** The ATS Resume Checker supports PDF and DOCX files up to 5 MB.

**Important distinction:**
The **ATS Score** is different from the **Match Score** shown on the Match Internships page.

* **ATS Score:** Measures the resume's ATS optimization.
* **Match Score:** Shows how well the candidate's resume/profile matches a particular internship listing.

### Application Workflow

Whether applying from the **Internships** tab or the **Match Internships** tab, the application process follows these steps:

1. **Inspection:** Click on an internship to open its detailed overview.

2. **Review:** Click **Apply** to open a final review panel displaying critical role details.

3. **Cover Letter Options:**

   * **Skip:** Proceed without attaching a cover letter.

   * **AI-Generated:** Instantly generate a personalized cover letter tailored to the specific role and candidate profile.

   * **Custom:** Write or paste a manually crafted cover letter.

4. **Final Submission:** Click **Apply** to finalize and submit the application.

### Profile Page

The Profile page serves as the central control panel with four dedicated tabs:

* **Profile Details:** Choose to auto-fill details by uploading a resume, or manually enter/edit information and click **Save**.

* **My Resumes:** Displays all uploaded resumes—whether uploaded via the Match Internships tab, Profile Auto-fill, ATS Score page, or directly in the My Resumes tab.

* **My Applications:** Tracks all internship applications submitted by the user.

* **Settings:** Manage account security, view registered email, update password, or execute account deletion.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **How do I apply for an internship?**

  You can apply either directly through the **Internships** page using filters or through the **Match Internships** page by uploading/selecting your resume to get your top 5 AI-matched roles.

* **How do I check my ATS score?**

  Go to the **ATS Score** page. You can either select a previously saved resume or upload a new PDF/DOCX resume. Click the appropriate analysis button to generate your ATS score and resume optimization report.

* **What file formats does the ATS Resume Checker support?**

  The ATS Resume Checker supports `.pdf` and `.docx` resume files up to 5 MB.

* **What does the ATS score tell me?**

  The ATS score indicates how well your resume is optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems. The accompanying report provides feedback and recommendations for improving the resume.

* **Do I need a cover letter to apply?**

  No. You can skip the cover letter, write/paste your own, or click to create an AI-generated cover letter customized to the role.

* **Where can I see my saved resumes?**

  Go to **Profile > My Resumes** to manage all your uploaded files.

* **Where can I track my applications?**

  Go to **Profile > My Applications** to view every role you have applied for.
